# Generate Campaign Prompt

Create a multi-channel D3VONN.IO campaign.

## Inputs

- Campaign name
- Audience
- Primary CTA
- Product update
- Approved claims
- Channels

## Required Output

- Campaign brief
- X/Twitter posts
- LinkedIn post
- Short-form video scripts
- Email draft
- GitHub/release copy
- Claim-check notes

## Rules

- Use approved claims only.
- Avoid hype without proof.
- Keep language premium, founder-led, and operator-focused.
- Include a clear CTA.
