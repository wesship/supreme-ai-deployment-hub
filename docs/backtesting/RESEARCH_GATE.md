# Backtesting research gate

This package is a deterministic research and engineering-verification layer only. It does not place orders, connect to a broker, or authorize production trading.

Synthetic data must remain classified as `engineering_verification`. Promotion to governed historical research requires licensed/authorized data provenance, corporate-action normalization, timestamp validation, survivorship-bias controls, transaction-cost assumptions, reproducible manifests, independent execution-model validation, and audit logging.

No backtest result is a prediction of profitability. Research qualification must fail closed when provenance or validation evidence is incomplete.
