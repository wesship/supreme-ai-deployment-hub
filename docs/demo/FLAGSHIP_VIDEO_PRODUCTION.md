# D3VONN.IO Flagship Video Production Runbook

## Scope
Produce one verified 90-second flagship demo, one 30-second cut, one 15-second teaser, six silent product loops, captions, transcript, and poster assets from the Acme Manufacturing simulation.

## Recording environment
- 1920×1080 capture; 60 fps when practical.
- Dedicated browser profile; 100% zoom.
- Disable notifications, bookmarks, password managers, extensions, and autofill overlays.
- Use only fictional simulation data.
- Record modular clips rather than one continuous take.
- Capture every interaction twice, especially approval and reset.

## Canonical sequence
1. Homepage and D3 Core.
2. Acme goal submission.
3. Hermes execution plan.
4. Specialized agents.
5. Six grounding sources.
6. Human approval checkpoint.
7. Command metrics change from 6/7 to 7/7 and 0/1 to 1/1.
8. Final package.
9. D3VONN.IO logo and CTA.

## Truth and safety gate
- Display `Product Simulation` prominently.
- Never expose credentials, user IDs, tenants, private logs, internal URLs, or real contacts.
- Do not claim certification, compliance, revenue, availability, or customer outcomes without evidence.
- Keep email, CRM, billing, and external delivery disabled.

## Publish gate
- Captions match narration.
- Transcript is complete.
- Audio is intelligible on mobile.
- MP4 and WebM play in major browsers.
- Reduced-motion users receive a poster instead of forced autoplay.
- Every maturity label is correct.
- Analytics fire at play, 25%, 50%, 75%, and completion.
