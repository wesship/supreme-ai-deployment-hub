# Acme Manufacturing Knowledge Base

> **Simulation data — not a real company or customer.**

## KB-001 — Operations modernization brief

Acme Manufacturing is evaluating a two-year modernization program focused on connecting operational data, reducing repetitive reporting, and introducing governed AI assistance. Leadership wants visible task ownership, human approval for consequential actions, and an auditable record of automated work.

## KB-002 — Revenue operations interview notes

The revenue operations team spends approximately 18 staff-hours each week assembling executive reports from CRM, support, order-management, finance, and spreadsheet exports. Research for a new strategic account usually takes two to four hours and varies by analyst. The team wants repeatable research, source citations, draft outreach, and an approval checkpoint before anything is sent.

## KB-003 — Compliance policy excerpt

External communications must be reviewed when they contain pricing, contractual language, regulated claims, customer data, or commitments on behalf of Acme Manufacturing. Automated systems may prepare drafts and recommendations but may not send external messages or modify customer systems without an authorized human approval.

## KB-004 — Product and service catalog

Acme manufactures industrial sensing modules, predictive-maintenance gateways, and ruggedized control assemblies. Its service organization provides installation, equipment monitoring, and maintenance-planning support for distribution and manufacturing customers.

## KB-005 — Strategic account qualification model

A strategic opportunity is considered qualified when at least three of the following are present:

- More than 500 employees.
- Multiple operational locations.
- A documented modernization initiative.
- High manual reporting burden.
- Need for cross-functional knowledge access.
- Requirement for governed or auditable automation.

## KB-006 — Outreach style guide

Executive outreach should be concise, evidence-based, and specific to the recipient’s operational priorities. Avoid unsupported savings claims, guarantees, fear-based language, or statements implying a completed security certification. Recommended structure:

1. Relevant observation.
2. Operational implication.
3. D3VONN.IO capability tied to that implication.
4. Low-pressure invitation to review a demonstration.

## Expected retrieval evidence

The golden demo should cite at least four of the six records above. Every material claim in the executive brief should map to a source identifier. Simulated numeric values must be labeled as simulation data.
