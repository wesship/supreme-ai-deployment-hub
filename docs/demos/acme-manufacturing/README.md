# Acme Manufacturing Demonstration Pack

> **Simulation only.** Acme Manufacturing is fictional. All names, records, metrics, documents, outputs, and events in this folder are representative demo data and must never be presented as customer results.

## Purpose

This pack provides one repeatable D3VONN.IO “golden demo” for product videos, live demonstrations, onboarding, sales, documentation, and investor presentations.

## Canonical goal

> Research Acme Manufacturing, prepare an executive opportunity brief, draft a personalized outreach package, route it through compliance, request human approval, and generate the final deliverable.

## Story arc

1. Goal submitted to Hermes.
2. Hermes creates a seven-stage plan.
3. Specialized agents are assigned.
4. Approved tools and grounded knowledge are used.
5. The workflow pauses before consequential outreach.
6. A human approves, requests changes, or rejects.
7. The final package and audit summary are produced.

## Files

- `company-profile.json` — fictional company and contact context.
- `knowledge-base.md` — representative source material for retrieval and grounding.
- `golden-workflow.json` — deterministic workflow stages, agents, outputs, approval gate, and demo metrics.
- `recording-runbook.md` — exact recording order and safety checklist.

## Truth and safety rules

- Display **Product Simulation** prominently during demonstrations.
- Use only fictional names and `example.com` addresses.
- Never connect this workspace to production email, CRM, billing, or customer systems.
- Do not claim the simulated metrics are production telemetry.
- Do not expose credentials, tenant identifiers, internal URLs, private logs, or real account information.
- Reset the demo before every recording or live presentation.
