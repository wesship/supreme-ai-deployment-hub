# Acme Manufacturing Recording Runbook

> **Product Simulation.** Use only the fictional data in this folder.

## Preflight

- Use a clean 1920×1080 browser window at 100% zoom.
- Disable notifications and hide bookmarks, extensions, email, account IDs, and internal URLs.
- Confirm the environment cannot send external email or modify a production CRM.
- Reset all workflow stages to `ready`.
- Display a visible `Product Simulation` label.
- Confirm each product surface is separately labeled `Live Product`, `Beta`, or `Simulation` accurately.

## Canonical input

Paste exactly:

> Research Acme Manufacturing, prepare an executive opportunity brief, draft a personalized outreach package, route it through compliance, request human approval, and generate the final deliverable.

## Recording order

1. **Homepage / command layer — 15 seconds**
   - Establish D3VONN.IO and open the command interface.
2. **Goal submission — 20 seconds**
   - Paste the canonical input and submit it to Hermes.
3. **Hermes plan — 25 seconds**
   - Show all eight stages and their dependencies.
4. **Agent activation — 25 seconds**
   - Show Research Scout, Market Intelligence, Communications, and Compliance Reviewer changing from ready to running.
5. **Knowledge retrieval — 20 seconds**
   - Show KB-001 through KB-006 and visible citations entering the work product.
6. **Compliance review — 20 seconds**
   - Show the four checks and the recommendation.
7. **Human approval — 30 seconds**
   - First capture `request changes`; reset; then capture `approve and continue`.
8. **Command Center — 30 seconds**
   - Show eight tasks, four agents, six sources, one approval, and zero external messages sent. Label all counts `Simulation`.
9. **Final package — 20 seconds**
   - Show executive brief, outreach draft, compliance record, next actions, and audit summary.
10. **Closing logo — 10 seconds**

## Required shots for short loops

- Command Center activity and completion states.
- Knowledge citations and relationship view.
- Workflow stages with approval gate.
- Security/compliance review and audit record.
- Marketplace agent selection, only when the actual surface is ready.
- Movie Studio, only when the actual surface is ready.

## Approval package checklist

The reviewer must see:

- Executive opportunity brief.
- Evidence sources.
- Outreach draft.
- Compliance notes.
- Affected systems: `None — simulation`.
- Buttons for Approve, Request changes, and Reject.

## Publish gate

Do not publish until:

- No credentials, real people, real prospects, private logs, or tenant information are visible.
- Captions match narration.
- Simulation labels remain readable on mobile.
- Claims match demonstrated behavior.
- WebM and MP4 play in major browsers.
- Reduced-motion behavior shows a poster instead of forced autoplay.
- The transcript is available beneath the instructional video.
