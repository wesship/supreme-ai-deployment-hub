The package cannot become `ADOPTED` / `effective=true` unless all of the following are present:

1. Filed formation evidence in the nonprofit evidence vault.
2. Bylaws evidence in the nonprofit evidence vault.
3. A valid board resolution produced through the Gate 22 human governance engine.
4. An explicit authority basis.
5. An adoption timestamp.

Database constraints enforce this rule. No agent, service workflow, or browser client may manufacture legal effectiveness by changing a status field.
